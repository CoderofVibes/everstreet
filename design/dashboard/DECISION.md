# Dashboard direction: Tony's choice (Nov 5, 2026)

Tony chose **C ("Business cards and handover")** as the base, with these changes for the real build:

1. **Keep the handover prominent while it needs a decision.** The sequence from funded escrow to
   ownership transfer is one of the most distinctive things on the page. (When no sale needs the
   founder, the hero gives its room back to performance.)
2. **One larger performance chart.** The owner switches between the portfolio and a single
   business. Source labels and currency units stay visible; detailed records are one click away.
   (TUSD revenue and USD compute stay on separate views, never one axis.)
3. **Shorter business cards.** Each leads with what the business does, one meaningful trend and
   its next action. Detailed costs, customer breakdowns and readiness checks move to the business
   workspace.

Keep the light palette, and give the chart and the handover more room rather than adding
decoration.

A (cockpit) and B (editorial) are kept for reference.
