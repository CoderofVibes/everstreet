## What this slice enables

A seller proposes exact terms for a named buyer. Buyer funding is separate escrow, both parties
accept the same digest, and close transfers owner/operator authority while leaving customer
units, keys, holds and refund rights intact. Seller proceeds remain a claim belonging to the
seller. Cancel/expire/abort return escrow as a claim belonging to its buyer. No deal method
sends a payout; existing bounded claims perform collections later.

Two fixed-at-initialization modes: Direct closes atomically on the second matching acceptance;
Gated enters Closing and only its configured closer can finalize an exact bounded order before
deadline. The latter proves caller/terms enforcement only. Drain, artifacts, journal and service
continuity outside the contract are NOT proved by a synthetic closer. No UI or gateway in scope.

## Required corrections to the candidate

1. **Pin beneficiary records.** Current release_party_slot only checks owner/claim/pending.
   A proposed buyer can otherwise delete an empty record needed by future escrow returns.
   Explicitly prevent release of all records referenced by a live deal, including unfunded and
   Closing states. Handle buyer revision with zero escrow, storage payment for a new buyer,
   old-record unpinning and payer-preserving later release. Never rely on an unchecked unwrap
   after an allowed release. Prove cancel/abort/finalize remain allocation-free once funded.
2. **Economic categories.** Current party claims/payouts are all owner_withdrawals. Do not book
   returned acquisition escrow or paid sale price as operating profit withdrawal, customer
   usage revenue, or service refunds. Preserve fixed beneficiaries AND category through claim
   aggregation, partial collections if needed, failures, retries, stranded paths and duplicate
   callbacks. Use bounded category state and explicit separate observations. Adjust fixed-size
   saturation flags/storage tests. State exactly what is owed versus actually paid.
3. **Resolve table contradictions before code.** Candidate withdrawal prose and D5 differ.
   Define the floor enforced from seller acceptance until termination; no withdrawals in
   Closing; min_reserve always applies. Buyer-first acceptance cannot commit a seller to an
   unprotected floor. Funding after expiry must be returned (G6, omitted in method table).
   Revision invalidates both acceptances; decide conservatively how escrow with changed price
   is unfunded/refunded without stranding it. Cancelling/expiry/abort cannot pay the wrong buyer.
4. **Exact sequencing.** Candidate increments control_seq both in acceptance and entering
   Closing. Choose and document exact increments for each externally visible transition;
   close_order binds the resulting frozen value. Checked generation/deal/version/manifest/
   control counters and amount/deadline additions before mutation. Refusal tests must actually
   detect mutation; distinguish unit-VM panic behavior from receipt rollback.
5. **Storage is measured, not asserted.** Preallocate fixed operator/deal/outcome slots if
   used; account strings, Borsh enum variants and root serialization cannot hide growth.
   Respect intentional deposits and original storage payer. Demonstrate transitions and
   operator replacement at both short and64-character accounts. No claims about deployed gas.
6. **No authority bypass in success evidence.** Flagship100/20/80, active-hold continuity,
   old-owner revocation and away-and-back tests must call actual sale methods. Retain clearly
   labelled cfg(test) corruption helpers only where essential to fault injection; never use
   injected ownership as proof that sale methods work. v1/v2 remain behaviorally unchanged.
