# Publish and discover a digital product coherently

**Why (Tony's words):** Everstreet should "showcase their product" and bring builders, clients and
acquirers together, with digital AI tools/SaaS/services central.

## What the founder and customer see

A founder with a released digital product can prepare an understandable public business/product
profile from the studio/desk, review exactly what customers will see, and choose to publish it.
They do not have to invent a class-style legacy offer/title/date/place to satisfy a hidden gate.
A visitor sees a coherent digital business: what it does, who it helps, supported customer
products, accurate availability/terms and the existing route to use the product. Private sale
intentions and private build/runtime/customer data never become storefront content.

## Checked gaps

003u's report had to create a legacy offering to publish its digital fixture. store.publish()
requires offer.title and marks the legacy offer/ideas published. PublicPage still uses legacy
offer sections, though it already has a public customer-offers section. Home/listing can say
Shaping after a released service with customers exists. The new digital desk already separates
real product/release/offer facts from legacy stage. Connect this foundation to publication and
discovery without breaking existing class/service businesses.

## Bounded vertical slice

1. Short design first: explicit owner-edited public metadata, preview/publish semantics, stable
   public URLs, source of product labels and exact safe projection. Preserve draft versus public
   values; saving private work must not silently alter a live profile. Owner reviews edits and
   publishes them deliberately. Existing published pages and URLs remain supported.
2. Digital publication eligibility uses valid digital profile/product information, not fake
   legacy fields. It must not imply provider/wallet/NEAR wiring is live. A founder can publish a
   truthful coming-soon profile if that is already supported, but no working Use CTA without an
   eligible visible customer offer. Distinguish page published, release exists, offer active and
   service availability. Missing/unavailable data is explicit.
3. A purposeful public digital profile and discovery card: title, concise promise/audience,
   product family, concrete capability summary, honest next action. Lead with digital products;
   old class/service routes and mixed businesses keep their valid offerings. Avoid duplicate
   contradictory empty offer cards, workshop/date/place defaults and hardcoded Shaping labels.
4. Reuse existing customer offer/use routes and immutable released-product facts where safe.
   Never expose private conversation, draft brief, prompts, artifact internals/provider config,
   customer counts/costs without separate explicit disclosure, documents/results or unpublished
   products. Build a narrow public allowlist and bound lists/strings. No private acquisition
   listing, asking price, buyer identity or evidence grant content on customer discovery.
5. Publication does not publish a sale listing, alter runtime release/price/plan, sign anything or
   grant authority. Unpublish/retire should remove future discovery/new-use entry points according
   to existing rules while preserving customers' existing uses/results/units. Ownership change
   transfers business public content and editing authority, revokes the former owner's edits,
   and does not invent verification of external domain/provider transfers.
6. Make the public-profile editor/review reachable from studio and desk, with dirty draft/lost
   response/access-loss recovery, bounded payloads and actor/business/response fencing. Do not
   overwrite pending edits on a reload. Reuse patterns already proven in import/offer setup.

## Done when

A disposable fixture created through real routes goes from digital brief/release/offer -> public
draft -> exact review -> local publish -> visitor discovery/profile -> existing customer workspace.
Edit/save leaves live snapshot unchanged until publish; old owner cannot update after ownership
change; unpublish/retire retain customer history. Also test a legacy class offering and a mixed
business. Public APIs must exclude canary private fields; same for rendered HTML/data.

Use fictional fixtures only and provide a labelled isolated preview; this job implements the
feature and verifies local behavior, it does not publish Tony's real site. Meaningful finite tests,
npm check/webbuild; responsive1440/1280/390/keyboard review where tools permit; report exactly
what was verified. Local commit and stop for Astra review. P1/L after003u1.

No external public release/push/submission, mainnet, signing/deployment, new spending, live
provider/RPC/wallet/main wiring, real data, research access/assessment or Day One. M1/M2/M3/M4/
M5/M6 remain in scope; this slice does not complete the roadmap. The separately blocked evidence
permission browser test remains pending and is not part of this job.
