## Experience

In Acquire, give the buyer a readable account of what they control after closing, what existing
customers retain, and what external transfers still need doing. Explain the basis and freshness
of each statement. Separate a proposed sale from a confirmed handover and a historical receipt
from current authority. Link to existing exact terms, operating readiness and relevant checks.

Allow an explicitly scoped portable record containing the permitted evidence for that release
and handover. Provide a verification result explaining which supplied bytes and identities match,
which evidence is missing, and which claims require a fresh external observation. The record is
useful to a buyer without implying a legal title opinion or proof that a product is error-free.

## Implementation and acceptance

- Reuse existing ownership/operator generations, v3 intents/observations, release/check identities,
  customer-obligation summaries and scoped evidence exports. Inspect current implementations
  first. Do not create a parallel authority registry or broaden a buyer's grant.
- Define a bounded versioned evidence format: exact business/network/code/release/sale identities,
  provenance, observation time, included items, required omissions and integrity digests. A digest
  establishes matching bytes; provenance and live truth need independent evidence. No false
  signature, certification, or current-authority claim from a locally generated file alone.
- Viewer and offline verifier refuse unsupported schemas, changed bytes, inconsistent identities
  and missing mandatory evidence. Bound parsing and decompression; no scripts or fetched URLs.
- Grant revocation, actor switch and sale during export cannot disclose private data. Customer
  documents, raw credentials, signed transaction bytes and unrelated founder conversations are
  excluded. Explain that a previously downloaded permitted record cannot be remotely erased.
- Synthetic before/after sale, changed terms, stale authority, partial transfer and tampering cases
  exercise real routes and verifier; buyer sheet agrees with actual access checks. Actual browser
  review includes desktop/phone, keyboard and readable missing/stale states.
- Required checks/build, precise report and local commits. Keep external testimony/transfer and
  live-chain gates separate. No publication or remote upload is authorized by an export feature.
