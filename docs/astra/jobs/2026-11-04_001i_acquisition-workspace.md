# Acquisition workspace: clear terms, evidence and continuity

Tony's approved direction makes NEAR purchase and handover central. Build a composed acquisition
workspace after001h, preserving the actual authority model and all recovery controls. P1/L.
Read PLATFORM_VISION, EVERSTREET_UI_DIRECTION and AUTHORITY before implementation.

## What the person sees and does

Give the buyer a useful business overview, substantial source-qualified performance and a clear
way into scoped due diligence. Keep private acquisition activity separate from customer product
pages. Distinguish listing interest, granted evidence, exact sale terms and completed ownership.
Use the approved luminous identity, deliberate typography and precise technical details. Avoid
turning every section into an equally weighted card. Exact terms sit on opaque readable surfaces.

For a sale, show business identity, current verified state and one relevant next step together.
Use a main terms/evidence region and a quieter continuity region. At phone widths the meaningful
state and next step come first. Keep navigation stable; do not hide stalled/recovery actions.
The progress display needs explicit completed/current/waiting/blocked words and accessible state,
not only two similar colored steps. Opening a review is not recording, signing or accepting terms.

The continuity region explains three different facts: authority that changes, customer rights and
service records that remain protected, and external assets that require separate verified work.
Use actual available evidence. Never imply domains, code ownership, secrets, provider accounts or
legal entity/IP transfer automatically. Qualify retained operator permissions from the observed
contract version. Pending operational prerequisites must remain visible after ownership changes.

## Grounded baseline

Astra inspected the actual local synthetic acquisition preview on November4 at cdf1084 UI build.
Screenshot: docs/astra/acquisition-before-001i-2026-11-04.jpg. Present page is a long introduction,
five equal step boxes, a full-width evidence table and a separate record card. Business identity is
not prominent. Customer obligations and operator scope sit deep in the terms table. Completed and
current steps have similar styling; the second reads 'The exact version recorded here' before a
version is recorded. It needs to describe the next action without suggesting a completed fact.

Review this version only calls HandoverController.review(), setting client display state; record()
is separate. Preserve that separation and explain it clearly. Exact raw amounts are necessary,
but can have a human-readable primary value ONLY with verified denomination metadata. Never infer
decimals from an account name. Keep exact base units and asset identity readily accessible.

## Underneath and scope

Review Acquire, AcquireListing, AcquireRoom, Deal, Handover and buyer handover entry, relevant
shared financial charts and existing controller tests. Reuse001h data-series distinctions. Keep
recorded-intent identity, request idempotency, fresh evidence, version changes, expired/rejected
terms, actor fencing, minimal buyer receipts and seller loss of control intact. Do not rewrite the
contract or add a fake wallet/close action. No main wiring, provider or live chain execution.

  precision and recovery tests pass; meaningful behavior tests for changed interactions.
- npm check and webbuild pass. Report exact scope, local commit and pending live integration gates.
  Stop for Astra review. No full milestone claim based on composition or fixtures alone.
