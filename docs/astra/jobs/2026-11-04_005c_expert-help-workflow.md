## What a person does

A builder stuck on a product can find relevant help in Everstreet without writing a coding-agent
prompt or exposing their private studio. An expert can publish a deliberate service profile for
AI workflows, SaaS/API implementation, design, evaluations, NEAR integration or operations. A
founder reviews scope, skills, availability and stated pricing, then sends a specific help request
inside the app. Both can return to a persistent request thread, clarify the scope, accept or
 decline, record delivery and confirm completion. This is a human expert engagement, visibly
separate from the AI companion and from acquiring a business.

Profile publishing is opt-in. Start with explicit skills, a concise offer, availability, capacity,
pricing basis (fixed/hourly/quote; unknown stays unknown), and links to chosen public Everstreet
work. Label self-reported claims. No invented reviews, ratings, credentials, verification badge,
AI match confidence or customer count. An expert need not own a published business to offer help.
Use existing person/profile identity; do not create a parallel account system.

Directory search/filter gives reasons for matches using stated fields, not hidden AI judgement.
A contextual Find expert help entry from the Studio/Operate keeps the founder's business/project
context; only an explicit reviewed request shares the chosen summary. Private customer documents,
results, cost ledgers, source connections, credentials and whole conversations never attach by
default. No unsolicited external message, email, notification service or contact-list import.

## Persistent request and delivery workflow

- A private request snapshot records participants, explicitly chosen business/project, task brief,
  desired outcome, deliverables, optional timing and budget with currency/unit. The UI previews
  exactly what the expert receives. Avoid calling a budget an agreed price.
- Draft -> requested -> discussion/accepted or declined/withdrawn -> delivered -> completed;
  document valid transitions, who can make each, and how changed scope is reviewed. Persist an
  append-only event trail and messages/delivery notes. Delivery is the expert's claim; completion
  is the requester's acknowledgement. No funds move and no service contract is silently signed.
- Explicit idempotency and uncertain-response recovery prevent duplicate requests/messages/
  transitions. Draft typing while a prior request is in flight stays distinct from its snapshot.
  No replay of a paid or privileged action is introduced here.
- Permission to discuss a task is NOT platform operator authority. Acceptance grants no studio,
  customer, source, release, wallet, treasury or signing access. A later delegated-operator slice
  must request and verify narrowly scoped access separately. Link to this distinction when useful.
- Personal expert profile and unrelated messages remain personal. For a business-linked request,
  define a minimum transferable business work record and an explicit continuity/consent policy
  before coding. On ownership change, old owner loses business-administration actions immediately;
  newly appointed owner does not silently inherit the expert's personal conversation or credentials.
  Pause continuation requiring new parties, show the specific reconnect/review action. Do not
  simply keep a stale personal owner authorized by the request's creation-time identity.
