## What works end to end

A Gated sale through the configured composition, with each step taken by its own code:

1. The participants' wallets propose (granting the platform's metering account, Q1), fund and
   accept.
2. The seller's acceptance starts the closing. It is refused unless the closer capability is
   ready: an exact close_sale-only function-call key, a guarded contract and an allowance (Q2/Q3).
3. The handover arms from evidence, with no signer. Admissions are gated inside their write
   transactions, with database backstops behind them.
4. Customer uses already in progress drain: a reserved use is returned, a held provider call
   finishes and is charged.
5. Readiness is proved. An immutable order is written, its attempt persisted before broadcast,
   then signed by the closer's FC key.
6. The contract closes. The existing V3Intents reconciliation applies it through its system
   entry, the gate releases to the buyer, and the next customer use is served under the buyer's
   grants.

The same sale works across two actual processes: kill-and-takeover, and both processes live at
once. It also works on the actual page over the actual child: B-1 minus the held-use display.
