# Astra review of digital-transfer proposal v1

November 2. The candidate in `2026-11-02_003d-digital-transfer-proposal.md` is **not accepted
for implementation** yet. This is design reasoning against its stated transitions, not new
executed evidence. Keep the useful units/claims/freeze separation, but resolve these schedules.

1. **Conflicting replay is silently accepted.** The on-chain bitmap stores no fingerprint.
   A second signed payload with the same key/nonce is skipped as already charged even when
   its fingerprint differs. That contradicts the exact-request conflict requirement. Choose
   an explicitly bounded fingerprint/outcome window, or another scheme that rejects altered
   replays and gives truthful same-request retry results. Recalculate storage and batch gas.
   Old nonces outside retained history must never be accepted as a new charge.
2. **Registration does not uniquely establish customer authority.** Anyone can sponsor
   `register(...for_customer...)` while selecting the customer's use key. A sponsor must
   not acquire spending rights by registering first. Separate storage sponsorship from
   customer key authorization, or require a customer-signed installation with exact domain,
   business, plan and nonce. Define key-slot replacement/revocation and outstanding uses.
3. **Block height is not a unique incarnation.** Unregister/re-register in one block can
   recreate the same incarnation, and resetting the same use key can revive an old signature.
   Use a contract-maintained unique registration counter or equivalent non-reusable identity.
   Bind a monotonic key epoch to use signatures; prevent replacement from erasing replay
   protection or making authorized unsettled work uncollectible without the stated policy.
4. **Holds lack customer consent and uses are not hold-bound.** Owner/operators can reserve
   units without a signed use or customer-approved hold policy. Specify the consent and
   bounded lockup guarantee. A SignedUse has no hold id; settlement only checks its deadline
   is before the hold's serve-until. An expired authorization must not be carried into a new
   later hold and charged. Bind the reservation/use to exact policy/hold and expiry rules,
   including the deliberate settlement grace. Delivery remains runtime-attested.
5. **Callbacks can still grow storage.** The period map gains a record each month, including
   from payout callbacks and settlement, contradicting the no-growth/storage-exhaustion
   guarantee. Preallocate a bounded period ring or otherwise fund and prove all future writes.
   Also make customer-claim targeting explicit: `Claim{to, amount}` / `claim()` lacks plan id
   when the same customer has claims in several entitlement records. Preserve a fixed
   beneficiary and exact callback destination without scanning entitlements.
6. **Physical token-balance invariant needs an asynchronous boundary.** A successful FT
   transfer can debit the business before its callback reduces `held`. At that point the
   stated `ft_balance >= held` does not hold. Keep the internal bucket equation, and state
   the physical-balance relationship with in-flight debits or restrict equality to quiescent,
   consistently observed states. Test the actual receipt schedule, not an impossible atomic
   view across two contracts. Unknown callbacks remain explicit pending obligations.
7. **Observation ordering cannot be generation plus status rank alone.** Revising Accepting
   back to Proposed or opening a new deal after an abort keeps generation unchanged and
   lowers the rank. Include deal sequence/version/freeze epoch and a consistent chain block
   anchor. Specify views of terminal deals after deletion and if last_abort/last_close have
   been superseded. Runtime recovery must not rely on a record the contract already deleted.
8. **Startup and restore recovery are incomplete.** T0 admits admin only via T1 or T4, so a
   normal business that has never sold has no reopening transition. Conversely, restoring a
   pre-freeze backup loses the minimum observed height; a stale final RPC response could
   reopen seller administration while a readiness signature remains valid. Specify a durable
   anti-rollback anchor or a complete recovery quarantine with a sufficiently recent final
   chain observation; wall time or two equally stale nodes alone is not a proof. Cover
   multiple workers/replicas and readiness issuance across restore, not only one process.
9. **Claims and privacy language must match scope.** Work logs in the implemented studio
   belong to the business and transfer; private personal notes are not implemented. Avoid
   promising that all "studio chats" stay with the seller. Do not say a secret salt alone
   makes a publicly retrievable plan private. Model/provider/config compatibility and result
   quality remain trusted runtime assertions, distinct from on-chain economic guarantees.

Return a coherent revised design and the remaining tradeoffs. Prefer a narrower proved
protocol over retaining broad claims with exceptions. No code, signing, deployment or new
transactions are authorized by this review. A revised design still requires independent
review and unit/integration evidence before it becomes a platform guarantee.
