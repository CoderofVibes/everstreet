## What was built

1. **Contract** (`contracts/business-v3`, source only):
   - `get_plan_terms` and `create_plan_guarded(input, expected_control_seq, expected_plan_id)`;
   - every guard runs before any write, the quoted deposit included;
   - the quote is `PLAN_BYTES × storage_byte_cost`, with `PLAN_BYTES` the measured 90 bytes.
2. **Serve policy** (`server/src/servePolicy.ts`, D1/D2). One function serves inspection, offer
   creation, prepare and readiness:
   - `reserve = max(MIN_SERVE_MS, runtime timeout + execution margin)`;
   - `execute_by`, `submit_by` and `sign_by` are absolute, counted back from the signed
     `serve_until`;
   - usability is judged from the worst accepted block (defaults: minimum hold 450 000 ms, grace
     360 000 ms).

   The contract expiry `accept_by` is unchanged:
   - an unknown attempt keeps its allocation until then;
   - there is no first submission after `submit_by`;
   - an original reconciled too late to run is returned uncharged (`too_late_to_run`);
   - `plan_unusable` is reported in inspection, offer creation, prepare and readiness.
3. **The plan request** (SaleActions kind `create_plan`):
   - a pinned read with strictly decoded plan terms, checked for coherence with control;
   - rules, the serve policy and the derived grace are all checked; exactly the quote is
     attached; refused while a handover is open.
   - **Confirmed only by its own transaction** (the reviewed id and `plan_created`) **and** a fresh
     `get_plan` equal to the review at the reviewed generation. A lookalike never confirms.
   - A matched transaction ID is kept durably even when the readback is unavailable. A check still
     in flight when another confirms answers with the stored result.
   - Different plan inputs under one control identity give 409 `request_conflict`.
4. **Appointment:**
   - an explicit replacement mask built from the current slot, with SUPPORT kept by default and
     removed only as chosen;
   - confirmed against the reviewed caps;
   - the wording says "permission", with service readiness shown separately.
5. **Customer access** (`GET /api/businesses/:id/customer-access`, owner only): three steps from
   the owner's rows and one fresh reading, with:
   - the guided defaults: 600 000 ms when it fits, otherwise the configuration's minimum rounded
     up to a minute within the rules; when unavailable, the remedy names which ceiling is in the
     way;
   - the quote;
   - the operator slot with its explicit without-support alternative;
   - the offers and configured service readiness;
   - the newest unresolved or refused request.
6. **The page** (`web/src/pages/CustomerAccess.tsx`, in the Service workspace above Customer
   offer). It drives the existing SaleFlow: a verified preview, one claimed ask, a check by its own
   transaction. The form follows the 04:27/05:45 hierarchy:
   - price first, with the currency, the meaning of one use and a readable example;
   - Limits in their own section;
   - timing explained plainly, with its controls under Advanced;
   - the exact plan under details;
   - storage cost in readable NEAR, with the exact yoctoNEAR under details.

   Behaviour:
   - the draft survives "Not now", a closed wallet and a reload;
   - an unresolved request is shown apart, with recovery by its own ID;
   - a new plan after an unresolved request sits behind a warned disclosure;
   - disabled actions say why, and focus returns to the opening control;
   - the confirmed plan pre-fills the offer inspection, which is never skipped.
