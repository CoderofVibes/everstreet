## What was built

**Methods:**

| Method | What it does |
|---|---|
| `propose_sale(terms)` | The owner proposes; the buyer's record is created with measured storage if missing |
| `revise_sale` | The seller changes the terms |
| `fund_deal` | Escrow, through `ft_on_transfer`; anything not exactly right is returned whole |
| `unfund_deal` | The buyer takes the escrow back |
| `accept_sale(seq, version, digest)` | Either party accepts the live terms |
| `cancel_sale` | The seller or the buyer ends an unclosed deal |
| `expire_sale` | Anyone ends an expired offer |
| `abort_close` | Anyone ends a Gated close past its deadline |
| `close_sale(order)` | Gated closer only; not payable |
| `set_manifest(hex32, expected_seq)` | The owner sets the manifest pointer |

**Views:** `get_control` (owner, generation, `control_seq`, mode, closer, manifest pointer,
operators, and the live deal with the digest to accept) and `get_outcomes` (an 8-entry ring).

**The two modes are fixed at `new(…, closer)`:**

- **Direct:** the second matching acceptance closes in the same call.
- **Gated:** the second acceptance enters Closing. Only the closer may finalize, with an order
  naming the exact deal, version, digest, generation, closing control sequence, manifest and
  sequence, issued within 20 minutes and not before Closing started, before the deadline.

**What a close does:** owner → buyer; generation + 1; the operators become exactly the agreed
grants at the new generation; the price becomes the seller's sale-proceeds claim. Customers'
units, keys, holds, claims and refund rights don't change.

**What it does not prove:** the closer's own checks (drain, artifacts, journal, service
continuity). A synthetic closer acts by hand in tests.

## The job's corrections

1. **Pins.**
   - A live deal pins the seller, buyer and escrow-funder records: `E_DEAL_PINNED` in every live state, funded or not.
   - A buyer change is allowed only with zero escrow (`E_FUNDED_BUYER`). It creates the new buyer's record at the seller's measured expense and unpins the old one.
   - The old buyer's release refunds the deposit to its recorded payer; the test checks the created transfer receipt.
   - Terminate and finalize look pinned records up explicitly and never create them.
2. **Categories.**
   - Party records hold `profit`, `escrow_return` and `sale_proceeds` claims, and `claim_party(category)` collects one of them.
   - The new payout kinds `escrow_return` and `sale_proceeds` go back to the same category on failure and keep their kind when re-sent from Stranded.
   - New period counters: `escrow_in`, `escrow_returned` and `sale_proceeds_paid`, each with its own saturation bit.
   - A sale never touches `earned`, `customer_returns`, `owner_withdrawals` or `purchases_in`.
   - Owed money shows in `get_party` and `get_treasury` (`claims_total`, `escrow_total`); paid money shows in the period counters.
3. **Floor, expiry and revision.**
   - Every acceptance, and every close, needs `available ≥ floor`. From the seller's acceptance, withdrawals keep `available ≥ max(floor, min_reserve)`. Closing allows no withdrawals.
   - A buyer accepting first doesn't commit the seller: only the reserve applies until the seller accepts, and the floor is rechecked then.
   - Funding at or after expiry is returned.
   - A revision clears both acceptances. If the price changes, escrow returns at once to the buyer's claim.
   - Escrow always goes back to its funder, who is always the buyer.
4. **Sequencing.**
   - `control_seq` rises exactly once per successful control call, including a Direct accept that closes.
   - `closing_control_seq` is the value after the call that entered Closing, and `close_sale` requires order = deal = current.
   - Generation, deal, version, manifest and control counters, and the escrow, held and deadline additions, are all checked before any change.
5. **Storage.**
   - The owner is now a `FixedAccount` and the operators are 8 pre-allocated fixed-width slots.
   - `appoint_operator` is a same-size rewrite and still needs ≥ 1 yoctoNEAR (excess refunded). It replaces 003e1's charged `Vec` push.
   - The deal slot and the 8-entry outcome ring are pre-allocated.
   - Measured deltas are 0 for every transition, with short accounts and with 64-character seller, buyer, grant and closer accounts. The root encodes to 974 bytes at every owner.
6. **No authority bypass.**
   - `test_set_owner` is deleted.
   - The flagship path, active-hold continuity, old-owner revocation and away-and-back all call the sale methods.
   - The remaining direct state edits are labelled fault injections: a removed record, a leftover grant, a manifest change behind a deal, and counters forced to their limits.

## Tests

**The 38 prior scenarios still pass.**

- s06, s07, s13, the stranded/recreated-record test and the random owner sequence now use actual
  sales instead of injected owners.
- s14's pinned sizes change: PartyRecord +32 and Period +48, as designed.
- The administration test now expects zero-growth operator slots.
- Nothing was dropped.

**New tests (25).** Each runs in Direct and Gated mode unless noted.

- **G1:** 100/20/80 across an actual close, with a 400P price and a 10P floor.
  - At close: customers unchanged; the owner is the buyer at generation 2 with only METER2; the
    seller's proceeds are 400P; escrow is 0; `held` is unchanged.
  - After: METER2 opens and settles a hold with the customer's unchanged key, giving
    `consumed 24, free 76`. The old operator gets E_NOT_OPERATOR, and the founder gets
    E_NOT_OWNER for withdraw, plan, manifest, appoint and propose.
- **G2:** a hold open across the close is settled by the successor, not the old operator. Without
  a successor (Direct only, since a Gated close requires one), it is released after its deadline.
- **G3/G4:** a seller payout failing after the close becomes the seller's profit claim. One with
  no callback stays pending for 40 days, and the buyer's withdrawable ignores both.
- **G5:** unfund → escrow claim → collected (booked as escrow returned); re-fund; a duplicate
  funding is returned; unfund and cancel are refused while Closing.
- **G6:** funding returned for no deal, the wrong amount or price, the wrong version or deal, a
  non-buyer, the seller, a malformed message, or at expiry. Direct mode.
- **G7:** a revision clears acceptances. Replaying a v1 accept, or v2 with an old digest, is
  refused; the buyer can't change while funded; a price change returns escrow to the buyer.
- **G8, G8b:** every close-order field and timing refusal, an exact issue-age boundary, the
  deadline boundary, replay, and Direct mode's E_MODE. Gated mode.
- **G9:** abort after the deadline; escrow goes to the buyer's claim; a late close is refused;
  administration reopens. Gated mode.
- **G10:** away and back through two sales: generation 3, only METER at generation 3, the deal-1
  replay refused, both outcomes recorded, each claim with its own beneficiary.
- **G11:** purchases, refunds, holds, settlements, releases and a re-registration during a deal.
  `available` never falls, `control_seq` and the digest are unchanged, and the sale still closes.
- **G12/G13:** draining below the floor blocks both acceptances; after the seller's acceptance,
  withdrawing to exactly the floor is allowed and 1 more is not; E_CLOSING in Closing. A buyer
  accepting first doesn't bind the seller.
- **G14/G15:** E_DEAL_PENDING for plan, appoint, revoke, manifest and a second proposal. Manifest
  sequence checks; the digest binds the manifest; a manifest changed behind a deal is refused at
  close (E_MANIFEST). Gated mode.
- **G17, G17b, G17c:**
  - The only growth is a missing buyer record, charged to the proposer, who is recorded as payer.
  - With an existing record, a proposal needs only 1 yoctoNEAR and grows nothing.
  - An underfunded new record is a `should_panic`: rollback isn't modelled in the unit VM.
  - 64-character accounts and operator replacement change no storage, and the root stays 974
    bytes.
- **G18:** 9 deals (cancel, expire, close) wrap the ring in place.
- **G19:** a Direct close is one call. Direct mode.
- **G20:** expiry boundaries. A generation moved under a live deal (fault injection) is refused
  with nothing changed.
- **G21:** pins in each live state, the old buyer's release refunding its payer, and post-close
  release refusals.
- **Control sequence:** the exact rise per call; refusals, returned funding, customer activity and
  callbacks change nothing; the closing value is bound.
- **Counters:** deal sequence, control sequence, manifest sequence, deal version and generation at
  their limits are refused before any change. Direct mode.
- **Terms:** every invalid term (buyer identity, price, expiry window, close window per mode,
  capabilities, duplicate and too many grants, terms hash, Gated without METERING, the closer as
  buyer) is refused before any change.
- **Escrow returns** keep their category through failure and a detached stranded retry.
- **G22, random:** 2 modes × 3 seeds × 400 steps, interleaving propose, fund (sometimes wrong),
  unfund, accept (sometimes stale), revise, cancel, expire, close (sometimes a future issue time),
  abort, claims by category, withdrawals, purchases, refunds and callbacks.
  - Every success gets I1′, D1, D2, D4, D5, the closing-sequence check, S0/S1, P1/P4 and U1/U2.
  - Every refusal is checked for no prior mutation.
  - Successes by operation: `[45, 53, 30, 31, 37, 29, 19, 9, 3, 1, 27, 56, 177, 68, 124, 158]`, with 10 closes.

## Differences from the candidate

- **Pins** are new (correction 1).
- **Party claims** are split into categories, with new payout kinds and new counters
  (correction 2).
- **Acceptance** needs the floor for either party, and a price revision returns escrow.
- **`control_seq`** rises once per call, not separately for an acceptance and for entering
  Closing.
- **The owner** is fixed-width, a change the candidate omitted.
- **Close orders** have no `attempt` or `drained_admin_seq` field: nothing could check them.
- **Operator slots** now carry a generation each.
