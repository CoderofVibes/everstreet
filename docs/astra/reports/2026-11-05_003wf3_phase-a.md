## What the design establishes

All of it is grounded in source at `3651645`, with `file:line` references in the document.

- **Work crossing ownership (W1–W18).** Each row gives the durable record, the exact admission
  point and a policy: gate, finish, fence, cancel, return, reconcile or resume. It also gives the
  readiness evidence, and what isn't instrumented yet.
- **A gate that isn't a racy count.** A per-business row is checked inside every admission's own
  `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` write transaction, never in route middleware. Once it commits, admitted
  gated work can only shrink, so a readiness read after it sees everything.
- **The state machine.** `gated`, `ready`, `ordering`, `outcome_unknown`, `closed_observed`,
  `reconciled`, `released_to_buyer`/`released_to_seller`, plus a non-terminal `attention`. The
  gate opens **only** on the contract's `closed` (with the existing reconciliation) or `aborted`
  outcome, never on a timeout, lost reply or expired order.
- **Readiness at one pinned read.** Every customer item is final, delivery included. No
  evaluation or trial work is in flight. `ent_held_value == 0`, the floor and escrow hold, the
  contract checks pass, and there is time before the deadline.
- **CloseOrder.** `issued_at_ms` is the read block's time. Dispatch is recorded before
  broadcast. Preparation, dispatch, transaction result, proven close, reconciliation and
  customer readiness are kept apart.
- **Why replacement orders are safe.** Every order of one handover closes the same deal, and the
  contract closes it at most once. The only dangerous action is opening the gate to the seller,
  which needs the `aborted` outcome.
- **The closer.** A function-call key for `close_sale` only, following the pinned near-api-js
  key-management reference (hash verified). It reuses the customer writer's transaction core
  with its own attempts table, is disabled by default, and reports truthful capability and
  observed health.
- **UI states mapped to APIs.** Migrations are additive. Budgets are bounded.
- **Test inventory.** Rust C-1 to C-6, encoded-call model M-1 to M-3, services S-1 to S-21,
  actual processes P-1 to P-5, browser B-1 to B-4.

## Limitations stated in the design

- **L1:** the contract lets the seller's own wallet, or another current-generation METERING
  operator, open holds during Closing. Readiness detects holds at its read but can't prevent one
  opened afterwards.
- **L2:** a stuck runner can delay readiness until an honest abort.
- **L3:** the 10-minute minimum close window is tight, so the default is 60 minutes.
- **L4:** all evidence is synthetic.
